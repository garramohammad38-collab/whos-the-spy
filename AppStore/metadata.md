## Description (en)
Who's the Spy? is a party game for 3–12 players on one phone.

How it works:
• Pick a deck and type your names
• Pass the phone: everyone secretly sees the word, except one player who sees "You're the spy!"
• Take turns asking each other questions about the word, without giving it away
• When time's up, vote for the spy
• The spy gets one last chance to guess the word from 8 choices

Decks: Football (Today's Stars, Legends, Arab Stars, Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, Saudi League) and Party (Animals, Countries, Food, Jobs, Things, Arab Celebrities, Movies & Series, Cartoons).

• 1,000+ words in Arabic and English
• Discussion timer (2–8 min) or no timer
• Scores across rounds
• Works offline, no account, no ads

Free: Today's Stars and Animals. "Spy Plus" unlocks every deck with a weekly or monthly auto-renewing subscription. Cancel anytime in your App Store account settings.

## Review notes (en)
Party game played on a single phone passed between 3–12 people. No login. To test: tap "Today's Stars" (free), tap Start, then tap the red button on each player's turn to see the word (one player sees "You're the spy!"), then Vote, pick a player, confirm, and pick a word as the spy. Locked decks open the subscription screen (weekly / monthly auto-renewable, no trial). Restore and offer-code redemption are on the same screen.
